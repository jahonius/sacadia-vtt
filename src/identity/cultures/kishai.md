# The Kishai


The thrumming of crackling metal makes hairs stand on end as the City of Kuchilaani approaches the plateau. Cunei merchants back away as the floating iron giant lurches to a stop over the packed plateau. Tribal diplomats in woven grass clothes descend to pick at the offerings made by the merchants of Whitecliff. After a day of haggling, the Kishai city spills tons of raw iron onto the plateau, takes its offerings, and leaves.

## The Skydancers

The Kishai are a tribal people inhabiting the dangerous lands of Kish. They are characterized by:

- **Kish:** Kish is a twisted arcane land of magic, razor-sharp grasses, sharp enough to cut through flesh with a light breeze. The radioactive effects of the Grass Font permeate the lands, and any who enter the grasses are corrupted by the Font.

- **Skyships:** The Kishai dwell in giant skyships - metal floating ships from a bygone era - upon which they build densely populated grass hut cities. The Skyships collect iron shavings from the grasslands below through a magnetic effect, and the Kishai sell these metal shavings to nearby cultures for produced goods.

- **The Spinelords:** Only brave hunters and priests enter Kish for religious customs, sticking to the long, ridgelike mountain peaks that pierce the grasslands and reach towards the heavens.

- **Cicadites:** The Kishai are embroiled in constant war against the Cicadites - font-corrupted humanoids who have lost their humanity, and now fight from a hive city that they have claimed.

## The Sea of Blades: History of Kish

Much of the history of Kish is hidden, obscured still beneath the blade-like grasses of the plains. The Kishai, superstitious and content, avoid the stories of the old world, but the Elosine Engineers living amongst them do their best to record what they can.

### The Old Olem

The lands of Kish were once the verdant bronze age farmlands of the East Olem Empire, a second age civilization ruled by coastal kings to the south of Kish. The verdant planes were dotted with townships, now hidden, under the shadows of eight floating, still rocks that loomed in the sky over Kish. Though stable, the East Olem fended off the advances of northern mountainous tribes they called the Kias, who regularly raided villages for food.

### The Fontspill

At the center of Kish, the gate to the Interstition was once called the Hole by the Olem, who poorly understood its alien functions. When the Fontspill occurred, a font fell through the hole, and landed in a patch of grass. Delayed by the strange depths of the Hole, the Olem thought themselves safe, but after several decades, grass began creeping from the Hole, ceaselessly progressing, until it slowly consumed the once-verdant farmlands of the East Olem Empire, starving them out. One day as this slow apocalypse came for the East Olem, the rocks floating over their planes lurched into motion, shaking off centuries of inertia and raining rock from the sky. They began to move across the planes. The East Olem did not find their way onto these ships. But the Kias did, lunging from their mountain ridgelines onto the ships using nets and rope. Doing so kept the Kias alive through the grassy spread of Kish. From this high vantage, they watched the Olem die.

### Skyship Failures

Over the many centuries, the Skyships have begun to fail. Though the Kishai have done their best to survive, even collaborating with foreign Elosine to attempt to engineer makeshift solutions, the skyships are failing. In recent decades, two have crashed, and one now flies dangerously close to the ground. The end of the Kishai is nigh.

## Geography of Kish

### The Grasses

The grasses of Kish have consumed the wide plains of this open region, obscuring many secrets beneath the blades. The grasses of Kish are as varied as grass itself. Massive forests of bamboo clank against one another in a cacophony of sound. Clouds of locusts blanket the horizon, eating the grass as quickly as it grows. Forever growing at the outer edges of Kish is the blades—razor sharp, tall growing grass that cuts through armor when a slight breeze rustles it.

### The Heart of Kish

At the center of Kish is the heart: a 20 square mile hole in the earth, beyond which lies an Interstition—a portal to the worlds outside worlds. Out of this hole crawls, around every edge, the twisting roots and tendrils of the grass font. The radiating energy of the Grass Font, corrupting and potent, leaks from this maw within the earth, and causes Kish to exist.

### The Ridgeline

A low, jagged ridgeline of mountains breaks several times above the sea of blades across the planes of Kish, stretching east to west. It is this jagged ridgeline of clay-brown earth which serves as the agricultural home of the Fontborne of Kish, who toil before their corruption takes them to provide for Kish in small, dotted colonies of terraced, scraggly crops.

### The Skyships

Five Skyships of Kish now remain, where once there were eight. Held together through the will of a fading people, and the engineering of a tinkering group of visitors from lands beyond the Ardus Yauga, the five Kishai Skyships hold the majority of all Kishai in their hands, keeping them afloat literally and metaphorically. Each Skyship is a different shape, and bears a different name. All have at their heart a Heartstone—an ancient engine of raw divinity which powers the magnets keeping the ships afloat. Surrounding that core are the ruins of temple cities—ancient neolithic cities that far predate the Kishai, now tombs to their ancestors. The Kish, visitors to these ruins, consider the city tombs sacred places, but do not deign to delve too deeply into the mysteries of the ships, preferring instead to colonize the surface and exterior hull.

# Cultural Tapestry

## Architecture

- **Grass Huts:** The Kishai build thatched grass huts by weaving the grasses and bamboos of Kish.

- **Shapes:** The seven shapes of the Kishai skyships are sacred to them, and they incorporate these shapes into architectural design for notable places.

- **Land and Sky:** Most Kishai live in densely populated Skyship homes, built several stories tall and built both atop the Skyship hulls and into their complex engines.

## Arts and Music

- **Flutes:** The Kishai have massive flutes affixed to skyships that play by channeling wind. The cacophonous drone they generate is a music they use to signal to one another. Smaller versions of these flutes are used by individual Kishai.

- **Weaving:** The Kishai do not use much pottery, but are expert basket-weavers, and they weave unique and complex basket-ware.

## Customs and Language

- **Language:** The Kishai use the language of Kish, their own complex language that strongly relies upon the vocal fritative. Few speak other tongues.

- **The Hunt:** The Kishai see meat as a sacred food, and difficult to acquire. Special hunts send Kishai hunters into the grasses of Kish in search of animals, and they only return to the sky cities when their hunt is complete.

- **Isolationism:** The Kishai are very isolationist and private. They do not wish to share their Skyship customs, and fear the outside tribes they see coming to take their goods. They broadly refuse to see foreigners unless those foreigners can help them maintain their skyships.

## Economy and Trade

- **Iron:** The Kishai trade iron collected under skyships with nearby cultures (the Olganyar and Cunei). The trade goods they acquire are shared, as the Kishai do not have a strong concept of personal ownership.

- **Internal Trade:** Internal trade amongst the Kishai is barter-based, and offering to perform acts of service factors heavily into the trade structure. Amongst the Kishai, doing something is considered more valuable than having something.

## Family Structure

- **Family Size:** Kishai families are quite small due to the size of sky ships, and the Kishai take great pains to ensure that their population remains under a threshold that would impact skyship population limits.

- **Clans:** Kishai live in small sky city communities, the largest of which is under 10,000 people. They typically see everyone in their city as part of their family, led by the Patriarch (their societal leader). On the ground, exiled corrupted Fontborne communities are even smaller (no more than a few hundred people).

- **Age:** The Kishai believe in greater wisdom as you grow older, and the oldest among Kishai are seen as helpful for their offered wisdoms.

## Folklore and Superstitions

- **Corruption:** The corruption of Kish is ever-present, and changes many Kishai (especially hunters who enter the grasslands). This corruption is seen as being touched by their God, and is viewed as an honor as well as a danger. When a Kishai fully becomes a Fontborne, they are ceremonially exiled to a ground community along a spine, where they live amongst other Fontborne.

- **The Earth and Sky:** It is believed amongst the Kishai that someday, the sky (their God) will finally give in to the flirtatious advances of the earth, fall in love, and fall to the earth, killing everyone. Thus, the Kishai strive desperately to keep her hating the Earth God. Shooting stars are seen as a sign that they must redouble their efforts.

## Food

- **Meat:** Meat is difficult to acquire and often in Kish contains the corruption of the Grass Font, changing those who eat it. Thus, meat is only eaten during special ceremonies in Kish.

- **Crops:** Kishai Fontborne exiles grow wild seed crops along rocky mountain spines, growing them by taking advantage of the verdancy of the Grass Font. These crops are then collected and offered to the sky cities.

## Games and Leisure

- **Skyjumping:** The Kishai practice a dangerous sport called skyjumping, whereby ropes are dangled from cities and hunters jump from the city to the lakes and rivers below, then grab a rope to climb back up. If they are left behind, they will be stuck unless they can reach the next Skyship route quickly enough.

## Government

- **Leaders:** The leader of society is a powerful gerontocratic Patriarch. Typically these patriarchs are ancient and weathered wise men who assist in leading society.

- **Ship by Ship:** The government of Kish is organized by skyship, with each skyship forming its own governmental entity. Each is treated as a separate, but generally peaceful, tribe.

- **Ground and Sky:** The Fontborne exile tribes of the ground make offerings to all skyships, which are seen as keeping the Sky Goddess from falling in love with the Earth God. In turn, the tribes of the sky make sacrifices to the sky goddess and tell her how ugly, hideous, and awful the Earth God is, so that the sky does not fall to the earth.

## Religion

- **Deities:** The Kishai believe in two primary deities: The God of Chaos, Life, and the Earth and the Goddess of Order, the Sky, and Death. The God of the Earth is said to have created the Cicadites, while the Goddess of the Sky created the Kishai. Both are worshipped.

- **Grass Font:** The Kishai see the grass font as the God of Chaos trying to impress the goddess of the Sky into coming down to him, and this is both a blessing and dangerous curse for Kish. The Cicadites are seen as holy warriors of the Earth God.

- **Altars:** Altars to the Sky Goddess are ropes surrounding a concentric ring of stone atop a high mound of dirt or a cliff. Altars to the Earth God are hole-like wells in the ground. Prayers and sacrifices are made at both, and these ceremonies are also an opportunity for Fontborne exiles and Skyship denizens to come together.

## Societal Structure

- **Revered Ones:** Certain members of society are revered for their work above and beyond the otherwise egalitarian structure. These people include Priests, Hunters (or anyone who descends into Kish), the Corrupted (those touched by the arcana of the Grass Font), and the Elderly.

- **Gerontocracy:** The structure of Kishai is supported by a gerontocracy, where the elderly and their opinions are strongly held.

- **Corrupted Nomads:** Tribesmen of Kish who are too corrupted begin to lose their humanity, becoming more wild, dangerous, and animalistic. These hunters are ritualistically offered to the earth god, and often form loose-knit tribes of corrupted nomads who live isolated lives on mountain ridges.

## Values

- **Community:** The Kishai value community and the continued operation of their skyships above all else, and they are very communally knit.

- **Secrecy:** The Kishai are incredibly secretive, and rarely even speak with outside foreigners. Only designated dignitaries are allowed to speak with outsiders, and they are trained to maintain the Kishai secrets.

## War

- **War Customs:** The Kishai use woven grass shields and spears when they enter the grass forests of Kish, as well as flutes to communicate commands in war.

- **Cicadites:** The Kishai are at war with the Cicadites - powerfully corrupted Fontborne created from the Grass Font, who have become a bestial, hive-like civilization that consumes all in its path.

- **Power of Kish:** Neighboring cultures fear the potential of a Kishai ship coming to attack them, because the aerial advantage would be completely uncontestable by nearby iron age cultures.

## Plot Hooks

- **The Hive City:** One of the cities of Kish has been corrupted into a Cicadite Hive, and the Cicadites pour from it to invade Kishai Fontborne along the world spines, as well as to attack other Kishai cities. If something is not done about the Cicadite Hive, it has the potential to destroy the fragile balance of Kishai culture.

- **A Hunt:** Traversing the lands of Kish is a dangerous proposition for anyone, as Kish is a rare biome connected to the Grass Font. Those who would travel inside of it will experience dangerous threats.

- **Trade:** The trade potential of trading with Kish for iron provides an incredible incentive for nearby cultures, and many play a dangerous political game to best maneuver themselves to receive the irons of Kish.
