# The Myrgha


The scents of a thousand rare spices waft through the marketplace of New Myrghos, exciting weary travelers who have come here for exotic western wares. They have haggled their way through licensed bureaucratic offices for days or even weeks to be here, but now they may finally trade their wares and procure the strange, often miraculous trade goods of the barbarian lands beyond.

## Wreathed in Gold

The Myrgha are at the height of empire, saddling the southern rim of the Ardus Yauga. They are known for:

- **Divine Origins:** The Myrgha claim descendance from their God Gongshuan, a demon lord who was murdered by his brother. The Myrgha ancestral families claim descendance to the twelve sons of this Demon God.

- **The Trickle Up:** The Myrgha pay tribute to Thirteen Divinely Ordained Oligarchs, with a focus of bringing wealth and glory to these twele. In turn, these Oligarchs protect those who pay tribute to them - and punish those who don’t.

- **Legalism:** A complex Legalistic Bureaucracy enshrouds Myrgha law and religion in kind. Legalist bureaucrats both enforce the will of the Oligarchs, and feign reigning it in. The Myrgha Bureaucracy, intentionally byzantine, supports the complex market empire created by the Oligarchs.

- **New Myrghos Markets:** At the center of the Capitol City of New Myrghos lies a famous sprawl of markets, selling the culture and traditions of nearby barbarian cultures - the Ikenni, the Olganyar, the Tianqi. The New Myrghos Markets contain a myriad of strange, divine, rare, and dangerous goods. The dance of the Myrgha economy can be felt here.

## Empire at the End of the World

The Myrgha are the dominant power of the Ardus Yauga, having clawed their way for centuries from nothing into a world class naval power. Myrghos, the island and coastal regions occupied by the Myrgha, is dominated by twelve Oligarchic, self-serving families, ruled by semi-divine Oligarchic rulers served without question by their people.

## A History of the Myrgha

The History of the Myrgha is a history woven through the Ardus Yauga and surrounding regions.

### Three Demons

At the beginning of the Fontspill, three demons escaped a prison through the Interstition that would become the Grass Font. These three demons, Tallemar shapeshifters once designed for war among gods, now siblings adrift in history, were consumed with a hatred of the divine, and wished to create a mark upon the world through blood. Each of these siblings made an empire of the native tribes that lived along the edge of the Ardus Yauga. The first, Shemura, went far to the west, creating the Ikenni. The second, Fengshuan, went to the eastern border of the Ardus Yauga, and founded the Empire of Fengxi. The third, Gongshuan, went south, and founded the Myrghos. Conflict was quick to follow between Gongshuan and Fengshuan, and nearly a hundred years after their escape, Fengshuan killed Gongshuan and scattered his twelve children to a western outcrop of rocks called the Soren Rou.

### War and Nemesis

For nearly two centuries thereafter, the descendants of the twelve children of Gongshuan, the Oligarchs of the Myrgha, embroiled themselves in a determined conflict against Fengshuan and his kingdom. Through the development of novel naval tactics and shipcraft, the Myrgha eventually killed Fengshuan and claimed his city of Goldenstone. The war left an indelible mark upon all Myrgha, and carved the high point in Myrgha culture.

## Myrghos Unique Locations

### The Markets of New Myrghos

The markets of New Myrghos, capitol of the Myrgha Empire, are the largest of the Ardus Yauga. In these markets, the Myrgha have made a reputation by selling rare, strange, and exotic goods from the ‘savage lands’ to the west - that is, they sell trade goods from the Olem Islands, the Ikenni, the Kishai, the Cunei, and even the Olganyar. They also make their own goods of great quality (such as garum and rare dyes).

### Riches of Goldenstone

Traders attempting to sail from the east to the Ardus Yauga must pass through the port of Goldenstone, which heavily taxes ships in and out of the two naval passes. The city of Goldenstone, forever a great symbol of wealth, brings great gold to the Oligarchic Charnei family who owns it.

### The Grand Lighthouse

The northernmost city of Myr Torel, the home of the Oligarchic Itthoa family, houses the Oligarchs with the greatest influence over the Cuneises. The Itthoans have achieved great wealth and power through their network of fishermen and Garum producers, and mark this wealth with an enormous lighthouse, made of black and white marble, that symbolizes their great power and wealth.

# Cultural Tapestry

## Architecture

- **Marble:** Myrgha architecture (especially wealthy architecture) heavily incorporates black and white marble from the Cuneises, as it exists naturally in massive quantities in Myrghos. This is commonly placed in a checkered pattern.

- **Bronze Domes:** Myrgha cities, as a display of wealth, adorn their buildings in domes made of bronze. The capitol buildings of New Myrghos are adorned in gold.

- **City Blocks:** The small, rocky Myrgha homelands of Soren Rou restrict lateral city growth, and so Myrgha cities are constructed using tight-spaced city blocks in tenement house patterns. Colonies with broader spacing opportunities still tend to follow this pattern.

## Arts and Music

- **Ostentations:** The most famous of Myrgha instruments are the Ostentations. Each one differs, but the concept is that each oligarch displays their wealth by having a massive instrument requiring multiple people to play. These people dedicate their lives to creating complex instruments and playing them.

- **Great Horns:** the Myrgha create vast brass horn instruments. These blare throughout each city at certain times of day, and are also used in times of war to send commands to soldiers.

- **Wealth:** Much of the art in Myrghos is dedicated to displays of wealth for Oligarchic families. Families which serve Oligarchs often house displays of Oligarchic wealth gifted them by their Oligarchs.

## Customs and Language

- **Black and White:** The Myrgha have a strong custom of black and white checker patterns, and legally mandate all Myrghos ships to sail black and white checkered sails.

- **Language:** The Myrgha speak Bushiu (the traditional language of the tribes and cultures of the Ardus Yauga)

## Economy and Trade

- **Unique Economy:** The Myrgha have created a complex economy designed to funnel gold directly to Oligarchs, and in exchange, provide value in the form of tributes and trade vouchers to those beneath the Oligarchs. Trading goods more directly to Oligarchs provides greater value than trade performed further from them, as a result.

- **Myrgha Trade:** The Myrgha have a vast network of trade and work very hard to expand it with their naval fleet. Predominantly, they take advantage of western civilizations’ general lack of understanding of complex economies to buy goods for cheap, then sell them for a great profit to eastern empires.

- **The Poor:** The Myrgha claim by way of pride that there are no poor, but in their economic system where wealth moves upwards, their people are constantly impoverished. Impoverished peoples are ‘removed’ from soceity by being transported by Oligarchs to external city-states, working mines, fishing, or farming as indentured servants.

- **Legal System:** The legal system of the Myrgha is byzantine, and with respect to the economy designed to encourage trade with external traders while also expanding Oligarchic wealth. They are ruthless at legalistic nickeling and diming of foreign traders.

## Family Structure

- **Patriarchy:** The familial structure of the Myrghos is predominantly patriarchal, but women hold a prominent political role as well, especially in intriguing games of familial wealth and politicking.

- **Servitude to Elders:** The familial structure of Myrghos is one of joyous servitude, and among most of Myrghos it is desired to be a better servant of one’s elder family or, even better, Oligarchs.

## Folklore and Superstitions

- **Folk Fae:** The Myrgha have several folk fae. Most notably, creatures called coin mimics (which look like coins but eat real gold) and tilepacers (small, raw, gold or silver carapaced crabs that can teleport, and have a back shaped like a square black or white tile). Both are worth a great deal of money if caught.

## Food

- **Seafood:** The Myrgha diet is predominantly based on seafood and imported goods. Fresh seafood is a critical part of each day’s diet for them. Imported goods make up the bulk of wealthy or exotic foods.

## Games and Leisure

- **Colosseums:** Myrgha enjoy sporting events as massive displays of wealth, and their colosseums are capable of multiple forms of sporting. Most often, Myrgha have mock naval battles, but they also drain colosseums to display footsoldier battles between Myrgha and mercenary soldiers from other countries, demonstrating conquests and events from history.

- **Parties:** The Myrgha host ostentatious parties to display wealth. Leading women figures in Myrgha Oligarchic families host these parties, with complex political games arranged often.

- **Gambling:** Gambling in Myrghos is forbidden, as it reduces wealth that could move upwards. It is considered morally wrong.

## Government

- **Oligarchs:** Everyone in Myrghos is beholden to one of the twelve Oligarchs who lead civilization, and they serve at the dictatorial whims of these Oligarchs. The sole exception to this is the Five Bureaucracies, which sit outside the Oligarchy and are designed to limit Oligarchic control.

- **Five Bureaucracies:** There are five Bureauracies in Myrghos which sit outside the Oligarchy:
  - **Policy:** In charge of creating laws and interpreting laws. Complex legal codes.
  - **Economy:** In charge of economic codes and collecting taxes. In charge of the judgement of criminals.
  - **Measurement:** In charge of measuring distances, measuring taxable wealth, and ‘measuring justice’ (Myrgha police force is part of this).
  - **Education:** In charge of education and furthering the Myrgha legal code. Runs post offices, which are used to monitor citizens and foreign traders alike.
  - **Religion:** Contains religious priesthood for Myrghos, dedicated to their religion.

## Religion

- **Rochi:** The Myrgha predominantly follow the Rochi Shamanic Order, a religion dedicated to the corpse of Gongshuan and the ancestry of Myrghos. Ancestor-worship takes a prominent central role in this religion, as does the worship of Oligarchs as “embodying the divine spirits of Gongshuan’s Sons”

## Societal Structure

- **Trickle-Up:** Myrghos is constructed around the twelve wealthiest houses of Myrghos, which are said to contain the spirits of the twelve children of Gongshuan, their divine demonic founder. The societal structure of Myrghos is therefore constructed around better serving one of these twelve great houses and supplying money to it. In exchange, the Myrgha Oligarchs are said to care for the families who serve them. Though this is not always true, there is a fierce competition between Oligarchs to have the most loyal families.

- **Oligarchs and Bureaucracies:** The Bureaucracies of Myrghos sit outside the Oligarchic structure and are not loyal to them. Oligarchs are meant to create laws that better the citizenry. They are often rife with corruption and wealth from Oligarchs.

## Values

- **Letter of the Law:** The Myrgha value the letter of the law above the spirit of the law, and their laws are byzantine and complex. They take great pride in their written laws and following them perfectly.

- **Wealth of Oligarchy:** Families value and pride themselves on the wealth of their Oligarch, and Oligarchic displays of wealth are valued across the Empire as points of immense cultural pride.

## War

- **Prominent Navy:** The Myrgha are some of the best sailors in the region, and have a vast navy as well as the most advanced military naval tactics, which they use to enforce their customs and trade agreements on their weaker rivals.

- **Mercenary Soldiers:** Myrgha leverage mercenary soldiers from a variety of countries and civiliations, giving them a cosmpolitan military.

- **In-Fighting:** The Myrgha, now at the tailing end of their imperial height, are experiencing a series of escalating in-fighting between Myrgha Oligarchs, as they begin to fracture and rupture over trade agreements.

## Plot Hooks

- **Civil War:** The Myrgha will one day collapse through a series of dangerous civil wars between Oligarchic families, as these proud Oligarchs fight over limited gold resources and trade control.
