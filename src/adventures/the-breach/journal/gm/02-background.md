# Background

## The Tianqi

Highly superstitious and grim, the Tianqi live difficult lives at the edge of humanity. They are deeply religious and wary of magic, yet their culture is bound up with the magic woven through their land. They believe in good against evil, and in their patriotism they are certain they are on the side of good.

Their culture is struggling under the weight of constant demon incursions. These take so much of their military strength that their other neighbors nip at their borders unchallenged. They are tired.

*The Tianqi are one of the rulebook's starter cultures (v1.2 pp.59–65): Binding Laws and Customs, the Heibrim Lore cultural talent, and cultural abilities and inheritance. The pregens follow them, and those abilities are in this adventure's Items folder.*

## The demons

Two hundred years ago, the Tianqi besieged their northern neighbors in the city of Qianpo, meaning to starve them out. The war between the two was so bitter that the Qianpo would not surrender even as they starved. They turned to cannibalism instead. The gods cursed them for it, and everyone who had eaten human flesh became a wild demon.

The Qianpo demons flooded across the land. In a panic, the Tianqi built Chuni's Wall, a vast wall separating the Upper and Lower Heibrim. North of it lie the city of Qianpo and its remaining demons; south of it lies the empire of the Tianqi.

*The rulebook adds that the demons of Qianpo are technically Folk Fae, not demons. Tianqi demon lore works on them all the same (Heibrim Lore). This adventure keeps them typed as Demons, as the one-shot does, so a Sentinel's Favored Enemy: Demons applies.*

## Chuni's Wall

Chuni's Wall is named for the general who proposed and built it. It is a fortified stone wall about 40ft tall, running between the Lake of Western Lights and the Lake of the Jiangshi. Military forts dot it at regular intervals and signal one another with smoke when there's an attack. The wall's guards make up most of Tianqi military strength, and they are trained to fight demons.

## The characters

The players take on the roles of the wall guards described on the {{page:players/the-characters}} handout. The combat can be very challenging, so if a character dies, that player takes the next unused character.
