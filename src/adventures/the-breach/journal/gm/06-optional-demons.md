# Optional Demons

Here are the other demons this module can throw at the wall, each more difficult than the last. The pregens are balanced around Wanabbul, so these are extra challenges. Roll {{table:which-demon}} for the one-shot's random demon, or pick one.

To swap demons, delete Wanabbul's token from the scene and drag the new demon onto the top of the map. Their tokens are already sized: Grubnut 3×3, Csenorras 4×4. Read the **Demonology** handout's facts for the new demon instead (its PD and rendable armor, its signature attack, and its size).

## Grubnut

![Grubnut]({{asset}}grubnut.webp)

{{actor:grubnut}} is a writhing, pustulous worm creature. He's less physically intimidating than Wanabbul, but faster: a 20ft move plus **Slam**'s 15ft carries him 35ft a turn.

**Behavior.** Unlike Wanabbul, Grubnut has no single focus. Above half health he won't move past the characters, and anyone standing in front of him gets his attention. Only below half HP does he race for the edge of the map.

**Grubnut and the wall.** He can't breach the wall instantly. On his first turn attacking it, the wall splinters; on the second it shatters; on the third he moves through. A Slam into the wall deals 5D10 to it and knocks Prone everyone within 30ft of the impact along the wall who fails a Finesse Check.

**Optional map change.** He moves much faster than Wanabbul. To make the fight easier, replace the oil barrel by the picnic table with a barrel of grease: knocked over, it creates a 20×20ft square of difficult terrain that slows him for a turn or two.

**Death.** He begins melting, leaving a strong stench, a sizzling corpse of boils and green ooze, and the outline of a dangerously warped human skeleton.

*In the system:* his two Pustule options are two abilities (**Pustule (Fumes)** asks which condition to give; **Pustule (Bile)** gives Hemorrhage). Use one per Pustule action. **Squirming Beast** makes him immune to Fatigue and Prone. For **Slam**, the 1X disadvantage on the Finesse Check is yours to apply.

## Csenorras the Manyworm

![Csenorras the Manyworm]({{asset}}csenorras.webp)

{{actor:csenorras}} is a giant writhing mass of black worms, loosely organized around a human skull somewhere deep within. It keeps a semblance of collective intelligence, but it is alien now.

**Behavior.** Csenorras is unpredictable and dangerous. In general it moves forward each turn, but once split, only one writhing mass presses forward; the others target individual characters to slow the party down. At the wall it splits in two: one part breaks the wall down over the next two turns while the other climbs the 40ft wall and immediately starts overrunning characters.

**Splitting and merging.** When it splits, both halves keep every condition the players gave it; when it merges, it keeps the maximum set of conditions from either swarm. Treat the halves as two monsters: a player with an ability that targets a single monster chooses which swarm it hits.

*In the system:* Csenorras's tokens are unlinked, so each swarm keeps its own HP and conditions. To split it:

1. Note its current HP and conditions, then delete its token.
2. Drag in two {{actor:csenorras_swarm_1}} tokens (3×3, 4 AP, dice two types lower).
3. Set each one's HP to half of what Csenorras had, and copy its conditions onto both.
4. For a second split, do the same with {{actor:csenorras_swarm_2}} (2×2, 3 AP). Those are too small to Swallow.

**Swarm Beast** makes it immune to the physical adversarial conditions, Prone and Blinded. Surrounded doesn't apply to it, and it can't be rended (its stat block's "PD 20(3)" is overruled by Swarm Beast).

**Death.** It shrinks as its health drops. When it dies, the last black worms wriggle weakly to the ground, leaving a single human skull stained black with ooze. Held to an ear, it murmurs faintly.

## The Weaver's Daughter

![The Weaver's Daughter]({{asset}}weavers-daughter.webp)

{{actor:weavers_daughter}} is the most intelligent, cunning and dangerous demon in this module. Her strategy targets the most powerful characters.

**Behavior.** She won't breach the wall. Instead she summons four {{actor:weaverspool|Weaverspools}}. Half of them go after the characters and try to sabotage the ballista; the other half make for the door and unlock it with an action, letting her wander in.

**Her true form.** She conceals it, appearing first as an old, frail woman so the players think the Weaverspools are the real threat. Below half HP she reveals her sewn-together maw, row after row of rending teeth (**Rending Bite** only works below half health). If she gets near the end of the map and thinks she has won, she takes the form of one of the characters with **Change Shape** and prepares to walk into the town beyond.

**Death.** She crumples into a pile of magic yarn in shades of blood red, black ichor and yellow bile.

*In the system:* Weaverspools are unlinked. Drag up to four onto squares next to her when she summons them. While she maintains **Bolster Swarm**, Weaverspools within 60ft get +2 to-hit, +1 Check DC and one more die type on Bite. Add those by hand, or with a temporary Active Effect.
