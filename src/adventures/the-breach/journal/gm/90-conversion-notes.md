# Conversion Notes

The Breach was written in 2024 against an early version of the rules. This adventure updates it to **Sacadia's Art of War v1.2**. Here is everything that changed, so you can put back anything you prefer.

## The heroes

The one-shot's character sheets were hand-simplified: abilities rewritten in plain terms, bespoke pools ("6 Call Points", "6 Trick Strike Points") and some abilities a level-5 character couldn't have. The pregens here are **rules-legal level-5 characters** built from the system's compendiums, so every ability on their sheets is the real one and the system automates it:

- level 5: Proficiency +3, 3 AP, **34 Combat Skill Points**, 6 Trait Points (no Trait above 2), 10 Talent Points;
- Tianqi culture: the *Heibrim Lore* cultural talent (Religion: Demon Lore 1) and the Binding Laws on each biography;
- their level features: Level 3 boon, *Rousing Success* with 1 Lore Point, and a Level 5 Mastery;
- HP from the book: profession HP × 5 plus Heritage HP.

The heroes' Check DCs are 15 (the one-shot said 14), from the v1.2 formula `9 + profession Trait + Proficiency + Courage/2`.

## Rules updates

- **Dying.** The one-shot's "3 dying levels, roll 1D20+Prof" is the old rule. In v1.2, a creature below 0 HP is **Wounded** and gains Battle Fatigue each turn, falling unconscious when that takes its AP to 0. It dies at negative half its maximum HP (p.230). The system runs this.
- **Quick rules.** The one-shot's quick-reference page is replaced by the v1.2 sheet in the player handouts. Notably, **Dodge** now needs Medium Armor and a Steadied reaction, and only works against ranged attacks (1D6 at level 5).
- **Healing Dates.** The rulebook prints two versions. This adventure uses the First Aid one (p.203): remove one Battle Fatigue, once per target per quick rest.
- **Fall damage.** Wanabbul's Grab and Drop keep the one-shot's 6D6. The book's general rule (p.245) would be 16D6 for 40ft. Change it if you want a deadlier drop.
- **Size.** The demons' "3X advantage against physical conditions" is the v1.2 size rule (p.224): Gigantic is three sizes above Medium. The system applies it automatically, so it isn't added twice. Grubnut's "1X" becomes the 2X a Huge creature gets.

## The demons

Monsters aren't built from player rules, so the stat blocks keep their numbers, with these conversions:

- **NPC stats** hold the stat block's full Trait Check modifiers (NPCs have no Proficiency in the system). Each attack's printed to-hit is reached with a bonus scoped to that attack. "Roll with NX Advantage" is a *Trait Expertise* passive.
- **Defenses.** The one-shot gives PD and MD only. TD is set equal to PD (it was one "physical defense" then), and AD to the lowest defense, which is what Chunrudar's *Imbued Fury* targets.
- **Rend.** "PD 19(3)" is a *Demonic Hide* armor item with 3 PD; rend comes off it. Csenorras can't be rended (Swarm Beast).
- **Damage types.** The Weaver's Daughter's *Force* damage is Mental damage in v1.2.
- **Speed.** NPCs now have a Move Speed field: Wanabbul 20ft, Grubnut 20ft (35ft with Slam), Weaverspools 30ft.
- **Grubnut's Pustule** is two abilities, one per check: Courage against Delirium or Nausea, or Finesse against Hemorrhage.
- **Csenorras's split forms** are actors of their own, with dice two types lower on the v1.2 dice ladder.

## Siege engines

The one-shot's player aides are now actors and Regions (see {{page:gm/battlefield}}): the ballistas and oil barrels are player-owned actors with their own to-hit, the pit trap is two automated Regions, and the wall walkway is a Region that gives defenders Height.
