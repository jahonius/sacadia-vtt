# How to Play Honnasusara

*Soldier 5 · Human (Talented) · HP 65 · PD 24 · Move 25ft · Check DC 15*

Honnasusara is a frontline ally and a tank. Stay as close to your allies as you can, especially after the wall is breached and everyone closes in on the demon: being near them puts them in range of your calls and reactions.

**Your attacks are the weakest in the party**, so let support come first.

| Attack | To-hit | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spear** (5ft, vs TD; can be thrown 40ft) | +6 | 1D6+3 Piercing |
| **Armored Buckler Bash** (5ft, vs PD) | +5 | 1D8+2 Bludgeoning |

## Your calls (6 Call Points, back after a quick rest)

Each call is an action that spends one Call Point.

- **Call of Healing:** you and every ally within 15ft gain 2D4 temporary HP.
- **Call of Respite:** up to two targets within 15ft make Trait Checks against one level of an adversarial condition each (you pick the condition per target).
- **Call of Fury:** spend up to 2 extra AP now. One ally within 15ft gets that many extra AP on their next turn. It works especially well on **Manchuthara**: 4–5 AP crossbow shots are her strongest.

## Reactions (one per round)

- **Courageous Command:** when an ally within 15ft makes an attack roll, add your Courage (+2) to it. Use it after you see the roll. **Selthimor** needs to keep hitting, so he benefits greatly.
- **Sacrifice:** when an ally within 5ft would take damage from an attack, take it instead.

## Drawing the demon's attention

- **Standard Bearer** (once, a Boost to your first action of the combat): try to give up to 3 levels of Taunt, split among enemies within 60ft (Courage Checks). Use it to make the demon target you, but be careful: you'll be in grave danger.
- **Imposing Figure** (action): try to give one enemy within 10ft 2 Taunt.

## Also

- **Stand By Me** (Focus): one ally within 5ft adds +1 to all their Trait Checks while you maintain it. Good against Draining Aura and Grab.
- **Rousing Success** (1 Lore Point, once): critically succeed at any roll, even after you've rolled.

Your **Red Glyph of Armor**, written in your own blood, gives +1 MD (already counted). Over a long rest you could move it to PD or TD; edit the effect on the item.
