# The Characters

> Chuni's Wall is a pale and desolate place. Your station here is an isolated and cold one, as you watch northward into the Upper Heibrim. Demons prowl the lands north of here. You have seen them in the night. They wander aimlessly, forever hungering for the flesh of humanity — hungry for you. It is a quiet and tense post. Until it isn't.

You are Tianqi wall guards: a stationed group of soldiers trained to fight the demons that prowl the Upper Heibrim. Two hundred years ago the gods cursed the cannibal city of Qianpo, and its people became demons; Chuni's Wall, 40ft of fortified stone, has held them north of the Lower Heibrim ever since. Tonight, one of them comes for the gate.

Pick one of the four heroes below. If yours falls, take the next unused one. Every hero is a level-5 Tianqi, bound by the Tianqi's Binding Laws and Customs (on their sheet's Character tab, under Identity), and knows the demons through *Heibrim Lore* (Religion: Demon Lore).

## Honnasusara the Soldier

![Honnasusara]({{asset}}honnasusara.webp)

An honored soldier who has manned Chuni's Wall since she was a youth. Honnasusara is human. She wears a breastplate emblazoned with the sigil of the Tianqi, a red and white flower over a blue painted background, and a proud glyphic sigil of protection sticks out from behind her armor: a protective totem for her and her warriors. *Tank and support.* {{actor:honnasusara}} · {{page:players/honnasusara}}

## Manchuthara the Sentinel

![Manchuthara]({{asset}}manchuthara.webp)

An accomplished warrior and the younger daughter of Honnasusara; a crossbowman and expert marksman. Manchuthara is human. She wears brown trousers tied at the waist and a straw cloak, and has long brown hair and deep brown eyes. *Ranged damage.* {{actor:manchuthara}} · {{page:players/manchuthara}}

## Selthimor the Thug

![Selthimor]({{asset}}selthimor.webp)

Newer to the group, from Tian Chun, a religious capital of the Tianqi. His job is to man the great ballista on top of the wall. Selthimor is a Mist Sylnfolk, with pale grey-blue eyes and hair and pale, off-white skin. He wears white trousers and a white shirt, and doesn't wear a cloak. *Melee damage.* {{actor:selthimor}} · {{page:players/selthimor}}

## Chunrudar the Fatebound

![Chunrudar]({{asset}}chunrudar.webp)

Chunrudar is intricately tied to this land: he made a pact with a demon of the mists for power. He wields it as a misty sword and by manipulating fate, fumbling his enemies. He wears blue, flowering robes and has short black hair. His eyes are a misty blue, though they were earthy brown before his recent pact. *Melee damage and debuffs.* {{actor:chunrudar}} · {{page:players/chunrudar}}

## Bringing your own character

You're welcome to bring your own character instead. Make them **level 5**. If they're Tianqi, choose the Tianqi culture on their Character tab. Its cultural abilities and inheritance are in the **Cultures** compendium.
