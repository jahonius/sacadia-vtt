# How to Play Chunrudar

*Fatebound 5 · Human (Craftsman) · HP 55 · PD 16 · Move 30ft · Check DC 15*

Chunrudar stabs his enemies with his sword. Play him as an up-close melee fighter once the wall has been breached.

| Attack | To-hit | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Greatsword**, your divine weapon (5ft, vs PD) | +6 | 1D10+5 Rot |

Your greatsword is your **divine weapon** (Fated Strike adds your Fate to its damage), wreathed in mist by your pact (**Elemental Weapon: Rot**). You can dismiss or summon it as a Boost to any action, and Rot damage overcomes physical damage resistance.

## Targeted Foe (once per combat)

A **Focus**: choose a target. While you maintain it (1 AP at the start of each of your turns), you get +1 to-hit and damage against it. You also get:

- **Imbued Fury:** your attacks against it target its **AD**, its lowest defense, which greatly increases your chance to hit;
- **Resilient Foe:** you resist all damage it deals you by 3;
- **Aware Foe:** 1X advantage on Make Trait Check actions against conditions it gives you;
- **Crushing Blow:** when you critically hit it, your greatsword's damage goes up one die type.

Be wary: maintaining the Focus costs AP every turn. It's best to wait until the demon has breached the wall and you're in melee range of it.

## Lucky Break (once per turn)

An action: up to 3 enemies within **30ft** make Fate Checks against your Check DC or take **1D6 Fumble**, which reduces their next d20 roll. It's like an extra action when the rest of your turn is Targeted Foe and stabbing, and it's useful early on while you can't reach the demon.

## Also

- **Rousing Success** (1 Lore Point): critically succeed at any roll, even after you've rolled.
