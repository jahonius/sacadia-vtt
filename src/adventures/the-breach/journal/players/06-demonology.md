# Demonology: Wanabbul the Vast

![Wanabbul the Vast]({{asset}}wanabbul.webp)

*Your GM reads this when the demon enters the fight.*

A gigantic shape looms out of the mist, as tall as the wall: a shadowy demon trailing whisps of black smoke. The old tales of the wall name it. Every Tianqi guard has heard of **Wanabbul the Vast**, and you know three things about him:

- **PD:** Wanabbul has **19 PD**, and **3 of it can be rended**.
- **Bite:** he can bite creatures within 5ft, rolling **1D20+12** against their PD. If he hits, he deals **2D10+5** damage.
- **Gigantic:** he can be **climbed**. Climbing him takes a Power Check at **1X disadvantage** (he counts as a slow-moving creature). Whatever you roll, rounded down to the nearest 5, is how many feet you can climb along him.

Want to know more? Roll **Religion: Demon Lore** (your Heibrim Lore), or have Manchuthara use **Favored Enemy**.
