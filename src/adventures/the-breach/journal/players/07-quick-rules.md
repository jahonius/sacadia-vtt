# Quick Rules (v1.2)

The full rules, and how the system handles each one, are in the {{manual:manual}}.

## Your turn

- You have **3 AP**. Most actions cost 1 AP and **exhaust a limb**: one arm, both arms, body, mind (your head), focus, or legs. Using a limb that's already exhausted costs **+1 AP**. The Abilities tab tracks it.
- **Move** up to your Move Speed (you can split it). **5ft Adjust** is a Boost on a turn you don't Move.
- **Focus** abilities cost a Focus action (1 AP) each turn to keep going. You can end a Focus at any time.
- **Help** (action): an ally within 5ft gets 1X advantage on one action you name.
- **Climb** (action): climb up to 5ft × your Power. **Drop** (action): drop up to 5ft × your Finesse safely. Drop further, or fall, and you land Prone and take fall damage.
- **Boosts** ride on an action (one per action, never on a reaction). Arm a Boost, then take the action.
- **Reactions:** one per round, used when it isn't your turn. **Steadying** (a basic action) makes your reactions stronger.
- **Lore:** Rousing Success costs a Lore Point and needs no AP. Use it any time, even after the roll.

## On Chuni's Wall

- The map has two levels: the **Wall Top**, 40ft up, where you start, and the **Ground**. From the wall top you see everything; on the ground, the wall blocks your view north.
- **Stairs** (east) take you between the levels: walk down them, or onto their foot from the camp, and confirm the level change at the foot.
- **Jump** into the hay or onto the tents: set your token's movement to Jump (token HUD), then move off the wall over them. You land Prone but unharmed. Each pile or tent works once.
- Standing on the walkway or the tower gives you **Height**: 1X advantage on attacks.

## Attacks

- Roll **1D20 + Proficiency + Trait** against the target's defense (PD, TD, MD, or AD, whichever the attack names). **AD** is a floor under every defense.
- **Frenzied** attacks are the regular kind. **Consistent** attacks spend extra AP on one roll: +1X advantage per extra AP.
- **Advantage** rolls extra d20s and keeps the highest. Height, Surrounded and a Prone target give 1X to melee attackers.
- **Critical hit** (natural 20): the base damage dice count as their maximum, or another critical effect your abilities offer.
- **Rend Armor** (action): a melee attack that deals no damage but rends 1 point of the target's armor.

## Basic reactions

- **Block** (Steadied): reduce a melee attack against you by 1D6.
- **Dodge** (Steadied, Medium Armor): reduce a ranged attack against you by 1D6.
- **Reaction Attack:** one melee attack against an enemy leaving your reach.

## Trait Checks and conditions

- A condition is resisted **once per level**: three levels of Pin means three Finesse Checks against the giver's Check DC. Each failure is one level.
- At the end of your turn, each adversarial condition drops by one level (unless it's Enduring).
- **Make Trait Check** (action): roll against a condition you have to shed levels early.
- Bigger creatures get advantage against your physical effects: 1X per size step.

## Wounded and death

- Below **0 HP** you are **Wounded**. At the start of each of your turns you gain 1 **Battle Fatigue**, which lowers your AP. When it would take your AP to 0, you fall **Unconscious**.
- Attacks against an Unconscious creature always hit unless the attacker rolls a natural 1.
- You **die** at **negative half your maximum HP**. If you fall, take the next unused hero.
- A **Healing Date** removes one Battle Fatigue: an action, a target within 5ft, once per target per quick rest.
