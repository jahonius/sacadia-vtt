# How to Play Selthimor

*Thug 5 · Fixerfolk (Mist Sylnfolk) · HP 61 · PD 16 · Move 30ft · Check DC 15*

Selthimor **hits harder every time he hits in a row**. Get up to the enemy and punch it as many times as you can. Each consecutive hit against the same target on your turn adds +1 to-hit (**Chained Advance**) and +1D4 damage (**Consecutive Threat**), up to +3 / +3D4. A miss ends the chain.

| Strike with your Fist Wraps (5ft, vs PD) | To-hit | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | +6 | 1D6+3 |
| 2nd in a row | +7 | 1D6+3 + 1D4 |
| 3rd in a row | +8 | 1D6+3 + 2D4 |
| 4th in a row (with Call of Fury or Help) | +9 | 1D6+3 + 3D4 |

**It's crucial to hit often.** Standing near Honnasusara (Courageous Command) or surrounding the enemy with your allies makes it easier:

- **Flanking Forces** (Boost): when you have a target Surrounded, your first melee attack against it each turn gets 1X advantage.
- **Drunken Fisticuff:** once per turn, when you miss with a melee attack, spend 1 more AP to re-roll it. It can save a chain.
- **Pained Bash** (action, your head): 3D4+2 damage. Thanks to **Tough Skull** it doesn't cost you HP, and after four hits in a turn, **Bloodthreat** adds your Consecutive Threat dice to it too.

## Also

- **Natural Wrestler** (action, once per turn): try to give a target within 5ft 2 levels of Wrestle Pin (Finesse negates). It's hard against creatures much bigger than you, but useful against the Weaverspools. **Kneejerk** adds a die type to your melee attacks while your target has exactly 1 Pin.
- **Lightning Shove:** 1X advantage on opportunity attacks, and creatures you Kick roll at 1X disadvantage.
- **Semper Steady:** your first reaction each turn is always Steadied.
- **Mist-born:** as a Sylnfolk of the mists, you resist Water damage by 3.
- **Rousing Success** (1 Lore Point): critically succeed at any roll, even after you've rolled.
