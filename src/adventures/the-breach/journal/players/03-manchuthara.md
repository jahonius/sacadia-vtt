# How to Play Manchuthara

*Sentinel 5 · Human (Resilient) · HP 50 · PD 15 · Move 30ft · Check DC 15*

Manchuthara is a **charged-attack** ranged combatant and the party's main source of ranged damage. Most of the time, stand at the east end of the wall and spend your whole turn firing **one** crossbow shot for maximum damage. You can keep doing that even after the wall is breached.

## The charged shot

Your **Crossbow** (120ft, vs TD) hits at **+5**, or **+7** with **Skilled Warrior** (once per turn, add your Wiles). Spend extra AP on one shot (a *consistent* attack) and each extra AP is 1X advantage. With **Boltshot** and **Pullback**, each advantage also adds 2D4 damage:

| AP spent | Advantage | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | — | 1D8+2 |
| 2 | 1X | 1D8+2 + 2D4 |
| 3 | 2X | 1D8+2 + 4D4 |
| 4 (with Call of Fury) | 3X | 1D8+2 + 6D4 |
| 5 (with Call of Fury) | 4X | 1D8+2 + 8D4 |

On the wall you have **Height**, which adds another 1X advantage, and with it another 2D4. Against demons, **Favoritism** adds +2 damage. At **4 AP or more** from the wall, **Crossbow Mastery** means the shot **hits even if it would miss**. Ask Honnasusara for her Call of Fury.

## Trickshots (6 points, back after a quick rest)

Each Trickshot adds your Wiles (+2) to that attack's to-hit (instead of Skilled Warrior):

- **Head Strike** (Boost to a ranged weapon attack): +2D8 damage if you hit. Use it every round.
- **Forceful Strike** (action): a ranged attack that deals no damage but pushes the target back 10ft. Use it to slow Wanabbul's advance.

## Also

- **Favored Enemy** (action): Wiles Check against a demon's Check DC to learn its to-hit, its Trait Expertise, or one of its abilities.
- **Pinning Bolt:** on a crossbow critical hit you may give 1 level of Pin.
- **Reactive Mind** (reaction): add your Wiles to a Trait Check against a mental condition.
- **Rousing Success** (1 Lore Point): critically succeed at any roll, even after you've rolled.
