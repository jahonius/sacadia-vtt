# Running the Game (GM)

## World settings

**Game Settings → Configure Settings → Sacadia:**

- **Combat resolution**
  - *Default:* damage from player characters applies automatically; damage to player characters
    waits for your click.
  - *Fully automatic:* damage applies automatically in both directions.
  - *Manual:* the system decides hit or miss only; you apply all damage yourself.
- **Condition stacking**
  - *Book* (default): a creature that already has a condition can't be given it again.
  - *Additive:* a house rule where new levels add to existing ones.

Players never see NPC defenses, DR, or Health in any resolution mode.

## Your private notes

Every attack and save sends you a private note with the numbers players don't see. It includes buttons
for:

- **Apply** (with DR), **No DR**, and **Undo** for damage, all setting Health to an exact value so
  you can click them in any order;
- applying or undoing condition levels;
- the attacker's **critical choice**;
- **after-the-roll reactions** (Block, Remaneuver, Black Rebuke …), labelled with who can use them;
- marking an NPC **Dead**.

## Turns

Start a combat in the Combat Tracker. Everything tied to a turn (AP refresh, condition decay, Hemorrhage,
the Insane table, auras, zones, Battle Fatigue, Focus upkeep) runs on **your** client as the active GM.
Keep one GM connected during combat.

## NPCs

- On an NPC sheet, **Paste Stat Block** fills in HP, AP, defenses, Check DC, CR, and Speed, and creates one
  ability for each entry you paste. An attack written as `melee attack vs PD, 1d10 slashing` becomes
  rollable; anything else stays as text for you to run.
- NPC defenses and Max AP are typed in directly. Set the **creature type** so Sentinel Favored-Enemy
  abilities recognize it.
- An NPC's **Speed** is typed in on its sheet (30ft when blank).

## Adventures

The **Adventures** compendium holds ready-to-run adventures. Open it, then open an adventure and click
**Import**. Its scene, actors, journals, items, and tables are added to your world in their own folders.
Importing again replaces them with fresh copies.

- **The Breach** is a level-5 combat one-shot for four players on Chuni's Wall. Start with its **GM Guide**
  journal, which covers setup, the four pregenerated heroes, the demons, and the battlefield. Its ballistas
  and oil barrels are actors every player owns, so anyone can crew them. Its pit trap is a scene Region that
  springs on its own.

## What you adjudicate

- **Movement:** any push, pull, knock-back, charge path, or "move up to your speed". The check that
  decides it is rolled for you; you move the tokens.
- **The battlefield:** Cover, Cornered, Obscured, Shadowed, Height, and Hidden (after a successful
  Sneak). Toggle them on tokens; the system applies their effects.
- **Zones:** moving them (drag the Region) and whether they were placed within range. You can delete
  a stray zone like any other Region.
- **Rend on NPCs:** a monster wearing no armor can't be rended. To let Rend reach one that wears
  armor, give it the armor as an equipped item. Its stat-block defenses already include that armor, so
  only the points rended off the item come off them.
- **Anything the card marks "GM applies":** Pull the Strand, and an Insane-table result chosen by
  Incredulous Will.

## If something looks wrong

- **A number is off:** every card lists the modifiers that applied and where each came from. Check
  the ability's dropdown settings (weapon family, chosen condition) and the token statuses (Raging,
  Steadied, Beast Form).
- **A turn effect didn't fire:** confirm the combat has started and a GM is connected.
- **Compendium changes:** after the system updates, re-drag changed abilities onto sheets. Items
  already on actors are copies and don't update themselves.
