# Conditions

## Adversarial conditions

Adversarial conditions (Pin, Hemorrhage, Panic, Fatigue, Fumble, and so on) have **levels**. The
**Stats** tab tracks them, and each appears on your token with its level.

- **Giving one:** the target rolls one Trait Check per level you try to give, and takes a level for
  each failure (see *Attacks, Checks and Damage*).
- **No stacking:** a creature that already has a condition **can't be given it again** unless the
  ability says it can (p.258). The system enforces this everywhere. When two parts of one action give
  the same condition (such as Jagged Blade's extra Hemorrhage), they count as one attempt. Abilities
  that say otherwise (Witch's Finger, Spring's Surge, a Witch's Poured Mold) are allowed through.
- **Conditions you give yourself do stack:** Remaneuver's Sting, Predator and Prey's Sting, and the Fatigue from Steed
  and Rider, Guard Them, Berserker Rage, leaving Insanity, Give of Thyself, and Transmute Trauma all add to the levels
  you already have.
- **More than 6 levels** are kept, but the condition's effects stop increasing at 6.
- **Losing levels:** each held condition drops by one at the end of your turn. **Enduring** conditions
  don't: Silenced, a condition made Enduring by Balanced Scale, Luckless Hold or Winter Frost, or Pin
  held by Wrestling Focus.
  Fumble clears completely at the end of your turn.
- **Shaking one off:** click the condition's **name** on the Stats tab to open the resist dialog.
  **Make Trait Check** rolls one check (+1 per extra AP), and each success removes a level. The
  **Incoming** mode rolls the checks for levels the GM asks you to resist.
- **Where it came from:** each condition remembers who gave it. That's how Hemorrhage from a creature
  with Mastery of Hemorrhage or Bloodthinner deals bigger dice every turn. Those upgrades are read from
  the giver when the Hemorrhage deals damage, so a Witch's Bleeding Bane counts only while she holds it.
- **Rests** clear most conditions.

## Rend and Corroded

- **Rend** removes AD, PD, TD, or MD from armor (p.258), never DR. Each level comes off a random point of a random piece
  of your worn armor or shield, finishing one piece before moving to the next; Rend Armor's choice and Clever Rend's pick
  go first. The Inventory tab shows each piece's rend, and a piece with nothing left is **Broken**. A creature wearing no
  armor, or with none left, can't be rended.
- A **quick rest** restores one point; a **long rest** restores all of it and repairs broken pieces.
- **Corroded** gives one level of Rend per level at the start of your turn. Once there's nothing left to rend, all your
  Corroded turns into Hemorrhage.

## Size and physical effects

Each creature has a **Size** (header). Against a physical effect (a physical condition, Prone, Dragged, a Kick), the
bigger creature rolls its checks with 1X advantage per size step, and the smaller with that much disadvantage, up to
3 (p.225). The save card shows it.

## Simple conditions

Simple conditions are on or off. Toggle them from the token HUD:

- **Prone:** your attacks at 1× disadvantage. Melee attacks against you get 1× advantage. You can't take
  reactions (v1.2); using one warns first.
- **Surprised:** attacks that hit you are critical hits. It ends when you take damage or at the start
  of your turn.
- **Blinded, Dragged, Unconscious, Insane, Raging, Steadied, Beast Form, Wounded:** as in the book.
  Many abilities check whether you have these, so keep them accurate.

## The battlefield

These conditions describe your surroundings (pp.254–255). The GM usually toggles them:

- **Cover** (Half or Full): set it with the **Cover** dropdown on your Abilities tab. Attacks against
  you are at 1× or 2× disadvantage.
- **Surrounded:** worked out from the tokens around you. Melee attacks against you get 1× advantage.
- **Cornered:** all attacks against you get 1× advantage.
- **Obscured:** +3 to AD, PD, MD and TD. Dim and Bright light make creatures Obscured.
- **Shadowed:** attacks against you are at 1× disadvantage.
- **Height:** your attacks get 1× advantage.
- **Hidden:** you can't be directly targeted. The system warns anyone who tries. You're revealed when
  you attack, force a Trait Check on someone, or take damage. Hide Behind Hide sets Hidden for you.

## Madness and Insanity (Oracle)

Madness is its own track from 0 to 6, and it *does* stack. Oracle abilities raise or lower it
automatically when used.

- Reaching **6** makes you **Insane**. You stay Insane until Madness falls back to **0**, and leaving
  Insanity gives you Fatigue.
- While Insane, you roll the **Insane table** at the start of each turn. Its HP loss is applied for
  you (Heather Root and Writhing Block reduce or cancel it), and the effect is shown on the card.
- Madness drops by one at the end of your turn unless something holds it (Voices That Shriek,
  Bloodlet).
- **Slightly Cracked** rolls are made at each rest and shown on your sheet. Arm the Slightly Cracked
  Boost to use one of them in place of a 1D3−1 Madness roll.
