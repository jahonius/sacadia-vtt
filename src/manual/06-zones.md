# Zones and Auras

## Auras that follow you

Some abilities affect everyone near you while you maintain them. These need no setup:

- **Blade Aura:** an enemy that starts its turn within 5ft of you (10ft with Bladius, 15ft with Greatius
  Bladius) gets a Finesse check card, with the damage already rolled.
- **Blackcloud** (v1.2): enemies inside your Clouded Foe make Trait Checks at 1× disadvantage.
- **Hallow's Calm / Hallow's Gale** (Magus): attacks made inside the aura are at 1× disadvantage (Calm)
  or advantage (Gale). The aura starts at 5ft and grows 5ft each turn you renew it, up to 5ft ×
  Proficiency. An Oracle starting its turn inside loses (Calm) or gains (Gale) a point of Madness.
- **Aura of the Grotto / Aura of Bane** (Witch): within 5ft × ½ Proficiency, your allies roll checks
  against adversarial conditions at 1× advantage (Grotto), or your enemies at 1× disadvantage (Bane).
- **Tempered Aura** (Witch): everyone within it is immune to Panic and Taunt, and loses any they had on
  entering.
- **Confusion** (Magus): no one else within 30ft (60ft with Distant Confusion) can start a new Focus.
  The sheet warns if they try.
- **Sphere Insanium:** at the start of your turn, every enemy within 30ft gains 2 Delirium, with no check.

## Placed zones

Some abilities create an area on the map: Stygian Abyss, Draining Aura, Voidsphere, Suppressing Fire,
Focal Point, God's Mist, and Pact of the Earth (30ft of difficult terrain around the Magus). To place one:

1. Use the ability as normal.
2. When asked, **click the map** where the zone goes. Press **Esc** instead to center it on your
   target, or on yourself if you have no target. God's Mist always centers on you.
3. The zone appears as a colored area, a Foundry *Region*.

After that, the zone works on its own:

- **Checks and damage.** A creature that **enters** the zone or **starts its turn** inside it gets the
  zone's check card or damage, at most once per turn.
- **Statuses.** Everyone inside Stygian Abyss is **Shadowed**; you and your allies inside God's Mist are
  **Obscured**. These are added and removed as creatures move in and out.
- **Attacks and terrain.** Attacks made from inside Suppressing Fire are at 1× disadvantage. Its area
  (and Stygian Abyss with Pooled Ink at 3+ Madness) is difficult terrain.
- **Size.** Madness-sized zones grow and shrink as your Madness changes. God's Mist moves to you at the
  start of your turn.
- **Upkeep.** Stygian Abyss costs you Proficiency HP on each turn it stands, including the turn you
  cast it.

## Swarm clouds (Hulinari, v1.2)

**Clouded Foe** and **Clouded Ally** spread your Swarm over X tiles (X = your Proficiency, +1 with Grey
Cloud, +2 with Stormcloud, more with Cloudsurge):

1. Use the ability. The tiles under your token are picked for you.
2. **Click** further tiles, each touching the cloud along an edge (no diagonals), until you have X.
   Press **Esc** to stop early.
3. The cloud moves with your token, keeping its shape. With **Rolling Fog**, it's laid on your target
   and follows them instead, and your own Move Speed is 0.

Enemies inside your Clouded Foe count as **Surrounded**; allies inside your Clouded Ally have **Partial
Cover**. Abilities that say "inside your Cloud" (Birdbite, Delirious Flock, Blackcloud, Birdshield,
Guiding Wingbeats, Armor Nics, Sacrificial Wing) read it automatically. Birdbite with nothing targeted
attacks every enemy in your Clouded Foe. Sharp Cloud and Dark Cloud ride the cloud while you maintain
them.

**How long a zone lasts:** a Focus zone (Suppressing Fire among them) lasts while you maintain the Focus. A zone
from a plain action lasts until the start of your next turn. All zones disappear when combat ends. To move a zone ("move the center 10ft
each turn"), the **GM drags the Region**. Whether you're allowed to place a zone that far away is up to
you and the GM.
