# Profession Notes

Most abilities just work once they're on your sheet. These notes cover the ones that need a setting or a
habit from you.

## Bladedancer

- **Named Weapons:** open a Versatile weapon's sheet and choose which Named Weapon ability it carries.
  You can have up to your Proficiency in named weapons. **The Vengeance** needs you to choose its
  enemy from the dropdown on its ability row.
- **Throwing:** Versatile weapons come with a **Throw** ability. With Wild Throws, Wild Strike asks
  whether you're swinging or throwing. Throws count toward I'll Come Back.
- **Exploding Weapon:** throw the named weapon at a target, and everyone else within 5ft of them takes its
  damage. Throw it with nothing targeted to pick a point on the map instead; everyone within 5ft of the point
  takes the damage.
- **Arrangements** spend Arrangement points. The movement itself is yours to make on the map.

## Fatebound

- Mark your divine weapon with the **Signature** toggle in your inventory. Divine-weapon abilities and
  Boosts check for it.
- **Glory** is spent from the pool beside your AP.
- **Targeted Foe** marks your current target. Abilities that care about your Targeted Foe (Crushing
  Blow, Imbued Fury) check against that mark.

## Hulinari Warrior

- Set your **Form** (Pack, Brute, or Swarm) on the Abilities tab. Toggle **Beast Form** on your token
  when you shift. Form-specific abilities only work in the right form.
- **Swarm (v1.2):** Hovering Foe is gone. Swarm abilities work through your **Clouded Foe** and
  **Clouded Ally** tiles; see *Zones and Auras*. Swarm points are now **Trickster Points**. A character
  built under v1.0 swaps the old Swarm abilities (Hovering Foe, Picking at Armor, Sharpbeak …) for the
  new ones from the compendium.

## Oracle

- Madness, the Insane table, and Slightly Cracked are covered on the *Conditions* page.
- **Pull the Strand** spends a saved Slightly Cracked roll. You choose + or −, and the GM applies it to
  the enemy's roll.
- **Soulbinding** and **A Boiled Leech** remember the targets you chose when you cast them.

## Sentinel

- Set your **Favored Enemies** on the Abilities tab. Abilities that care about favored enemies read the
  target's creature type.
- **Trickshot** points are spent from the pool. Variable spends ask how many.

## Soldier

- Toggle **Steadied** on your token when you Steady. Many Soldier abilities, Block, Dodge, and Grab
  depend on it.
- **Calls** spend Call points. Their buffs go onto the allies you target.

## Thug

- **Berserker Rage** starts your rage: you're marked Raging for Proficiency rounds (+1 each with Lengthy Rage and Anger
  Management), counted down at your turn ends. When it ends you gain ⌈Prof/2⌉ Fatigue after your conditions drop, so
  you still have all of it at your next turn (with Endurance Mastery, a Power Check card instead). While raging, your first melee attack each turn offers to be the free rage attack (no AP
  or limbs, no Boost).
- **Pin** from Natural Wrestler enables Harm and its Boosts. Wrestling Focus keeps your Pin from
  dropping at the end of turns.
- **Semper Steady** treats you as Steadied while you still have your reaction this round.

## Magus (prestige)

- **Spell Slots** are your pool (refilled on a quick rest). Scrollwriter, the Unfurlings, Mastery of the
  Tome, and Mastery of the Mind each add 3. Abilities that say "expend X Spell Slots" ask how many.
- **Your Tome** is an ability on your sheet. **Use it** to open its research record: tick the abilities
  you've researched (each is a Long Rest Action) and press **Record**. Adding a Tome ability that isn't
  recorded shows a warning. Prerequisites such as Astrology[3] are shown as text; checking them is up
  to you and the GM. The record lives on the Tome, so it travels with it.
- **Contract Tome:** choose the defense it raises in the dropdown on its row.
- **Blood for Bane** is a Boost to any action that spends a Spell Slot. Roll 6 or more and the slot is
  refunded.
- **Clotsnipe** asks how much extra AP to spend. Target every creature in the line.
- **Save-gated Focus** (Bane, Catnap, Crown of Insanity, Enemies Abound, Forbid Hallowing): the targets
  roll once, when you start it, and only those who fail are affected. Renewing it doesn't ask again
  (Enemies Abound re-checks, and a success ends it).
- **Law of Alliance:** damage to anyone bound is split evenly, and you take the odd points. **Death
  Ward** stops the next drop to 0 HP at 1 HP and ends the Focus. **Lawful Sanctuary** makes anyone
  attacking the warded creature roll a Wiles Check first; on a failure they pick another target.
- **Checkered Shield** and **Absorb Attack** appear as buttons on the GM's card when you're hit.
- **Summons** (Animate Dead and its upgrades) have no stat blocks in the addendum; the GM runs them.

## Witch (prestige)

- **Your Promise** is an ability on your sheet. **Use it** to mark it broken, or kept again. While it's
  broken, your Witch abilities warn before use until you take **Atone**. With Promise of Nonviolence,
  attacking or bleeding a creature that isn't on your side asks first and breaks it.
- **Divine Touch** is your pool (twice your Proficiency with A Full Well).
- **Ally Focuses** (Razorleaf, Shield Transference, Fourleaf Touch, Poured Mold, Bleeding Bane …): target
  the ally when you start it. Renewing warns if they're more than 5ft away. Options such as the weapon
  type or the defenses are asked each time you use it.
- **One-shot reactions** (Hallowed Touch, Tweak the Heart, Wellspring, Blossom): use them *before* your
  ally rolls. They apply to that ally's next temp-HP roll, condition attempt, or ability.
- **Two-target abilities** (Olive Branch, Transmute Trauma): target the first creature, then the second.
  For Transmute Trauma the second rolls.
- **Funnel Energy:** target both creatures (or just one, with Given Energy, and you're the other). You roll one
  Fate check per level of the tired creature's Fatigue, against their own Check DC. Each success removes a level
  and gives the other creature 1 AP on their next turn.
- **Greater Transference** strengthens Shield Transference only on an ally, not on an enemy through Foe
  Transference.
- **Poured Mold:** pick the condition on its row. While you hold it, that condition stacks on your target
  up to your Proficiency, and it's all removed when the Focus drops.
- **Nettle** asks which of the target's conditions to convert and what into.
- **Witch's Presence:** pick the condition on its row. Your side is immune while you're on the scene and
  conscious.
- **Balanced Fare** and **Harmed Fare** appear as buttons on the GM's card after a miss or a hit.
