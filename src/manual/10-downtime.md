# Downtime and Money

Between adventures, characters rest, work, and pursue their own ends (rulebook pp.263–266). Their money is on the
**Inventory** tab, their Influence on the **Character** tab, and a Long Rest is planned and taken from the **Long** rest
in the header.

## The purse

The **Inventory** tab's purse holds **gold coins** (gc; the book's "gp" too) and **silver coins** (sc).

- **Starting gold:** a new character with an empty purse gets a button for their profession's starting gold (Bladedancer
  20gc, Fatebound 10, Oracle 25, Sentinel 10, Soldier 5, Thug 30).
- **Change:** the arrows change a gold coin into silver; shift-click changes silver back into a gold. How many silver make
  a gold is a world setting (**Silver coins to a gold**, default 100). The rulebook prices in both and never says.
- **Prices:** items show their price under their name, in gold or silver (a Candle is 1sc). The **coins** control on an
  item's row sells one to a merchant for half its price (p.266), after asking; half of a silver is nothing.
- **Buying:** drop an item from a compendium on the purse to buy it (see **Building a Character → Personal goods**).

## Influence

**Influence** (p.264) is favor with a group: a guild, a temple, a noble family, a city's people, a ship's crew. Gain it
as a Long Rest Action (at most 2 a Long Rest). While the group is present, **Spend** a point for them to take action for
you, give you a hint about a lead, or get you a thing; the card asks the GM to say how. You can also add, rename and
remove groups by hand on the Character tab.

## A Long Rest

A Long Rest is at least a week of safe, uninterrupted downtime. Press **Long** in the header to open the Long Rest window:

1. **Plan each week.** Each week gives one Long Rest Action. Choose it, and fill in what it needs. **Add a week** for more.
2. **Take the Long Rest.** Each week resolves in order, and one chat card lists them all. Then comes a **Fitful Rest**
   (full Health, pools, Rend), and your **Lore points** refill to your Lore Limit (p.168).

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Earn Money** | An odd job pays by Proficiency: 10, 20, 60, 100, 200, 250gc (p.265). A business you inherited can pay instead: the Shrimp Trawler 60gc, the Restaurant of Forever Stew by its rarity, a Ministry 50gc … Suan of the Family triples odd jobs. Other work: enter what it paid. |
| **Acquire Influence** | +1 Influence with the group you name. At most 2 a Long Rest. |
| **Make an Offering** | Expends the item you offer (the Holy Tools' **Offering** or **Offering (Token)**, or anything else) and rolls 1D20 with a level of Advantage for your Religion specialization, each 100gc you offer (the item counts at its price: an Offering is 100gc), and each week given to it (more weeks of offering add to the one roll). At least a token offering (1gc) is needed. A natural 20 (or 30) gains a Lore point, once a Long Rest, on top of the refill. |
| **Pray for Guidance** | A Religion Check: 20 or more is answered, and the GM gives insight. Once answered, more prayer does nothing. The book asks for a **Holy Symbol** in a readied slot; the card says if there isn't one. |
| **Investigate a Lead** | The lead goes on the card for the GM. At most 2 a Long Rest. |
| **Harvest** | A random craft ingredient: the GM rolls on the Harvest table. At most your Proficiency a Long Rest. |
| **Craft** | What you craft goes on the card; crafting is with the GM (Chapter IX). |
| **Shop for Bargains** | Rolls the month's Bargain Sale and Bargain Buy (the kind of goods), and your Economy: Bargaining Checks for each: the deal, and how many items are on it (p.266). A Bargaining specialization adds its ranks. The items on deal are rolled from the compendia and linked on the card, each with its price and what the deal makes it: a Sale takes the deal off; a Buy is a merchant paying the deal over their usual half price. The printed table names an older edition's tables: Cooking Supplies are the kitchen furnishings, Adventuring Gear the tools, Miscellany the baubles, Armor Trinkets the jewelry and runes. Magic Items are the GM's. |
| **Train** | Removes one ability, talent or specialization rank, and its points come back to spend. An ability another of yours requires stays. |
| **Something else** | Anything you agree with the GM. |

Each Long Rest you may also swap up to one combat ability and one talent point for free (p.235): remove them from your
sheet and spend the points again.
