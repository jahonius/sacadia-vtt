# Welcome

This manual explains how to play *Sacadia's Art of War* in Foundry VTT with the Sacadia system: how to
build a character, take a turn, and what the system does for you automatically. Players can read the
first half; GMs should read all of it.

The rulebook is still the authority on the rules. This manual covers how those rules work **on the
table**: which button does what, what gets rolled and applied for you, and what stays in the GM's hands.

## What the system does for you

The system automates any rule that is a roll or a number:

- **Rolls.** Attacks, damage, Trait Checks, and saves roll with the right trait, Proficiency,
  advantage, and bonuses already added. Each result card lists every modifier that applied, so nothing
  is hidden.
- **Resolution.** Attacks are compared against the target's defenses on the GM's side. Players see
  *Hit* or *Miss*, never the NPC's numbers.
- **Conditions.** Condition levels, their effects, end-of-turn reduction, and the no-stacking rule
  are all tracked.
- **Your turn.** AP, limb exhaustion, reactions, Focus upkeep, usage limits ("once per turn"), and
  start-of-turn effects are handled as turns change.
- **Abilities.** Nearly every profession ability is wired up. Passives adjust your numbers on their
  own, Boosts ride your next matching action, and reactions appear as buttons when their moment comes.

## What stays with the GM

Some things stay with the table on purpose:

- **Movement.** Pushes, pulls, charges, and anything else that moves a token on the map are the GM's to
  apply. The *check* that decides whether it happens is still rolled for you.
- **Range and reach.** The sheet tells you when a target looks out of range, but it never blocks you.
- **Judgment calls.** Anything that depends on the fiction, like whether a creature is found, what
  counts as cover, or whether a spell gets through a Voidsphere.

## How warnings work

When something looks wrong (not enough AP, an ability already used this turn, a target out of range,
a condition that isn't met), the system asks **"Use it anyway?"** rather than refusing. The GM can
always overrule the automation.

## Where things are

- **This manual:** the **Sacadia User Manual** compendium. You can also open it from **Game
  Settings → User Manual**, or from any character sheet's header menu (**⋮ → Open the User Manual**).
- **Compendia:** abilities by profession (including the **Magus** and **Witch** prestige professions),
  **Lore Abilities**, Profession Masteries, **Profession Features & Legendary Masteries**, Basic Actions & Reactions, and
  equipment (weapons, armor, shields, adornments, personal goods, trinkets, home goods).
- **Rules version:** the system follows *Sacadia's Art of War* **v1.2** and the Prestige Classes
  addendum (v0.2-3).
