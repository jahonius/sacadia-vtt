# Attacks, Checks and Damage

## Attacks

An attack card shows your to-hit roll and damage roll. The GM's side compares the to-hit against the
target's defense (PD, TD, MD, or AD) and adds **Hit!**, **Miss**, or **Critical Hit!** to your card.
Players never see an NPC's defenses, DR, or Health.

- **Damage** is applied according to the world's **Combat resolution** setting. By default, damage from
  player characters is applied automatically, and damage to player characters waits for the GM. The GM
  gets a private note with the numbers, DR, and **Apply / No DR / Undo** buttons.
- **Criticals** happen on your crit range (natural 20, or lower with abilities). Surprised targets are
  always critically hit, and so are Steadied Opportunity Attacks. The GM applies your crit choice
  (Maximize, Critical Harm, or a condition) from the private note.
- **On-hit conditions** from the attack are offered to the GM to apply. If the target gets to resist
  ("Finesse check negates"), the target's player gets a **Roll Save to Negate** button instead.
- **Multi-attacks** (Wild Strike, Dagger Threat, Freefire) roll each attack separately against its own
  target.
- **Courage on weapon attacks** (v1.2 p.219): a weapon attack uses Courage instead of its usual trait
  when your Courage is higher. The card says so.
- **Untrained attacks** (p.6): each profession is trained in certain attacks (most in melee or ranged).
  An attack your professions aren't trained in rolls without your Proficiency, and the card marks it
  *Untrained*. The Witch is trained in none.

## Trait Checks forced on a target

When your ability makes someone roll a Trait Check, your card has a **Roll Save** button. The player
who controls the target clicks it with their token selected.

- A condition is checked **once per level**: trying to give 3 Pin means 3 checks. Each failed check is
  one level taken (p.258).
- **Advantage** adds dice and keeps the best. **Disadvantage** turns that many checks into automatic
  failures.
- **Fumble** reduces only the first check (p.257).
- Damage attached to the check applies on a failure. Some abilities deal half damage on a success.
- Optional spends such as Confidence, Fateful Saves, and Reactive Mind are offered before the roll.

## After the roll: reactions to attacks

Some abilities change an attack after it's rolled: Remaneuver, Lucky Strike, Block, Dodge, Please Don't,
Sacrifice, Black Rebuke, Checkered Shield, Absorb Attack, Balanced Fare, and others. When one of them could apply, a button for it appears on the GM's
resolution card, labelled with who can use it. Clicking it spends the cost and adjusts the result.

## Damage, DR and temporary HP

- **DR** reduces damage from each hit, but a hit that gets through always deals at least 1 (v1.2 p.192).
  Some abilities give extra DR against particular attacks, such as attacks on your MD or while you're
  Surrounded.
- **Shared damage** (Sacrifice, Sacrificial Wing, Law of Alliance): DR applies once, to the whole hit,
  before it's split. The shares don't apply DR again.
- **Damage types** (pp.184, 222): weapon damage takes the weapon's type (bludgeoning, slashing, piercing, imbued, all
  physical); elemental weapons deal their element, and some abilities deal mental damage. The card shows the type, and
  the target's **Resistances** for it (or its group, or `all`) add to its DR. Elemental damage ignores resistance to
  physical damage.
- **Temporary HP** absorbs damage first. It doesn't stack (you keep the higher amount) and it ends when
  combat ends.
- **Self-damage** from your own abilities (Mad Smear, Bloodlet, Stygian Abyss, Clotsnipe, Life
  Transference) is reduced by Mistletoe and Blood Mastery. Under a Witch's **Self Heal** it becomes
  temporary HP instead.

## Wounded, Dying and death (p.230)

- Below **0 HP** you are **Wounded**. At the start of each of your turns you gain a point of **Battle
  Fatigue**, which lowers your AP until you rest. With no AP left you fall **Unconscious**.
- Attacks against an Unconscious creature always hit unless the attacker rolls a natural 1.
- At **−½ your max HP** you die. The token is marked dead.
